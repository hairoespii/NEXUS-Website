Privacy Policy

{{draftNotice}}

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to do the work you ask for.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • This website uses no cookies, does not count or track visitors, and shows no ads.

Who is responsible

{{operator}} We are responsible for the information described here. Contact us at {{email}}.

What we collect

  • When you email us, we keep your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you send us, such as Amazon listing links, ASINs, or other listing details.
  • When you buy, payment is handled by Stripe. We never see your card number. Stripe shares with us the details we need to record and deliver your order, such as your name, email address, business name, billing country and postal code, the payment method you used, and for a card, its brand and last 4 digits, the amount paid, and the date. Stripe's checkout may also show a box, sometimes already ticked, to save your details, including a phone number, with Link, which is Stripe's own service for saving details, under Link's own terms and privacy policy. If you don't want that, untick it before you pay.
  • When you visit this website, we don't collect anything ourselves. The site has no forms, no cookies, no visitor counting, no ads, and no tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To do the work you asked for and send you the results.
  • To reply to your emails.
  • To keep the business and tax records we're required to keep.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse.

We don't send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who helps us

  • Stripe processes payments. Stripe also uses payment information for its own security and fraud prevention. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Google provides our email through Gmail. See Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts this website. GitHub says that when someone visits a GitHub Pages site, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor has signed into GitHub. See GitHub's Pages documentation and GitHub's privacy statement.

These companies are based in the United States, so your information may be stored or processed there. We don't sell or rent your personal information to anyone. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Orders. We keep order records and the emails about them for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Then we delete them.
  • Free samples that don't lead to an order. We delete the emails and listing details within 12 months.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We'll delete it, except records the law requires us to keep, which we delete once we no longer have to keep them. Email {{email}} and we'll reply by email. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local privacy laws, including the right to complain to the privacy regulator where you live, and we'll respect them.

We do not track visitors

This website doesn't track visitors, over time or across other websites, and doesn't let anyone else do that. A browser setting that asks sites not to track you doesn't change anything here.

Security

The site uses a secure connection. You pay on Stripe's checkout page, not on this website. Payment is handled by Stripe. We never see your card number. We never ask for your passwords.

Children

Our services are for businesses. This website isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If we learn that we have, we'll delete it.

Other websites

Links to Stripe's checkout, Amazon, X, and other sites take you to services we don't control. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a significant change, we'll also tell recent customers by email.

Contact

Privacy questions? Email {{email}}.
