Refund Policy

{{draftNotice}}

The short version

  • Try the free 5-listing sample first. It costs nothing.
  • Cancel before we start your check and you get a full refund.
  • If your report is late, or something is wrong with it, tell us. We'll fix it or refund you in full.

Free sample

The free sample checks up to 5 bundle listings and is delivered by email. It's free, you don't give any payment details, and nothing is billed afterwards, so there's nothing to refund. It's limited to one free sample per Amazon seller account.

Cancelling before we start

If you change your mind after paying but before we start reviewing your catalog, email us and we'll cancel your order and refund your payment in full.

If your report is late

If we don't deliver your report within the delivery time shown on the product page (or by a new date you agreed to by email), you can ask for a full refund.

If something is wrong with your report

If your report leaves out listings you sent us, or doesn't match what the product page describes, email us within 14 days of delivery. We'll fix the report. If we can't fix it, we'll refund your payment in full.

What isn't covered

  • Amazon's decisions. We don't control Amazon, so we can't refund because of what Amazon decides about your listings.
  • Later rule changes. We check against Amazon's policy as published on the day we review your listings. Changes Amazon makes after that aren't grounds for a refund.
  • Change of mind after delivery. A delivered report is finished work, so we don't refund it just because you no longer need it. If something is wrong with it, see the section above.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email {{email}} from the email address you used at checkout.
  2. Tell us the date you paid and why you're asking.
  3. We'll reply by email to confirm.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are always for the full amount you paid, and go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Once a refund is sent, your bank usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Please talk to us first

If something isn't right, please email us before contacting your bank. We'd rather sort it out with you directly, and it's usually faster for you too.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. The version in place when you paid applies to your purchase.
