What's changing on January 11, 2027

Amazon has announced an update to its Product bundling policy that takes effect on January 11, 2027. Under the update, most product bundles listed as a single ASIN must be packaged by the original manufacturer or brand.

Amazon lists exceptions, including bundles covered by a letter of authorization from the brands or manufacturers of all the products in the bundle, bundles in gifting browse nodes and camera product types, and bundles created with Amazon's Virtual Bundles tool (which Amazon says may not yet be available for every store, brand or fulfillment channel). Amazon is also removing some earlier bundle restrictions.

Amazon says bundles that are still noncompliant after January 11 will be deactivated and become listing violations on the seller's Account Health dashboard, and that it will notify sellers by email.

Sources: Amazon's announcement New requirements for product bundles starting January 11 on the Amazon Seller Forums (September 2026) and Amazon's Product bundling policy page. Last checked October 21, 2026. Amazon can change its policies at any time, so always confirm in your own Seller Central account.

What we check

  • Whether one of Amazon's listed exceptions may apply: a letter of authorization from the brands or manufacturers of all the products in the bundle, a gifting browse node, a camera product type, or a virtual bundle made with Amazon's Virtual Bundles tool
  • Amazon's bundle listing basics: the word "Bundle" in the title; a description that says it's a bundle and names every item; a first bullet point that says it's a bundle of a stated number of items and names them; and a main image that shows every item in the bundle and nothing that isn't included

Amazon says bundles that are still noncompliant after January 11 will be deactivated and become listing violations on your Account Health dashboard, and that it will notify sellers by email.
