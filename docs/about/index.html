About NEXUS Research

NEXUS Research is a small, independent online-services business. We find problems small businesses run into and sell simple fixes online.

What we do

We look for specific, practical problems that cost small businesses time or sales, and turn each one into a simple service with a clear scope and a fixed price.

Our first product, the Amazon Bundle Rule Check, helps Amazon sellers review their bundle listings against Amazon's updated bundling policy, which takes effect January 11, 2027.

How we work

  • Online only. No sales calls or meetings. Everything happens by email.
  • Prices up front. Every product shows its price before you buy.
  • Try first where we can. For example, the Amazon Bundle Rule Check starts with a free 5-listing sample.
  • No passwords. We never ask for your account logins or passwords.
  • Secure payments. Payments go through Stripe's checkout, so we never see your full card number.

What we are not

NEXUS Research is not affiliated with Amazon or any other marketplace, and we are not a law firm. Our work is not legal advice.
